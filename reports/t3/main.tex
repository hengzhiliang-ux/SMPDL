\section{Introduction: Remove a Law, Gain a Discipline}\label{sec:intro}
% Hook + three-act overview + claims list. FORWARD CALIBER ONLY here.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Hook}: among 29 removal experiments in the corpus,
        three grew an entire discipline where a law was deleted --
        convex analysis (after removing unrestricted duality), Sobolev
        theory (after removing regularity presuppositions), measure
        theory (after removing the Riemann integral's founders). The
        wound does not stay blank.
  \item \textbf{Three acts in one sentence}: collapse has a syntax
        (§4); recovery is parameter reset (§5); emergence type is locked
        at removal time (§6) -- validated forward (§7).
  \item \textbf{Claims} (each with evidence anchor; qualifiers kept):
        \begin{itemize}
          \item[C1] $\forall$-constraint laws collapse far more often
                than $\exists$-constructor laws (0/15 fragility;
                Fisher $p\approx0.013$, exploratory, family-error-rate
                marginal -- stated as such).
          \item[C2] Axiomatic load-bearing is weakly correlated with
                fame (proxy evidence only: ToC, $n=4/3$).
          \item[C3] Recovery products are re-wired new concepts, not
                copies of the removed law (R1--R6, all 29 groups).
          \item[C4] \textbf{Two-stage law (strongest claim)}: emergence
                type at removal time is locked by the functional type of
                the law -- \emph{forward 10/10} under a preregistered
                protocol, independent blind adjudication
                $\kappa=1.00$ (same-lineage, disclosed; cross-lineage
                §7.5), against a ``every removal is $\beta$'' baseline
                that breaks exactly where the theory predicts.
        \end{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Preregistration statement}: forward predictions
        registered and frozen before adjudication; adjudicators blind
        to predictions' provenance; full protocol in §7 and Appendix.
\end{itemize}

\section{Discussion}\label{sec:discussion}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Mirror of reverse mathematics (theorems $\to$ axioms vs
        axioms $\to$ emergence; Friedman--Simpson cited).
  \item Implications for AI mathematical reasoning: after a constraint
        is removed, an LLM should be able to \emph{predict the type} of
        what grows -- this framework supplies the criteria; adjudication
        pipeline as reusable protocol.
  \item Limitations, four items, verbatim from Memo §5 (not softened):
        same-lineage adjudication (mitigated, not eliminated, by §7.5
        cross-lineage); $n=10$ forward rounds (+3 extension);
        ToC proxy for load-bearing; Type-II circularity retired
        $\to$ H-endo signature family.
  \item Future work: S8 fresh-law forward rounds (live evidence during
        review); human adjudicator ($\kappa_2/\kappa_3$) recruitment
        ongoing.
\end{itemize}
