\subsection{Inductive-Closure Boundary Statement}\label{sec:act3-boundary}

\noindent\textbf{Statement (re-review action 3).} The
$\alpha/\beta/\gamma$ taxonomy and the functional-type criteria
(T1/T2/T3) are \emph{inductive products} of this corpus. The
preregistered protocol covers their \emph{application} --- predicting
the emergence type of a newly registered law before adjudication ---
not their construction. Consequently: (i) in-corpus fit is not
evidence of out-of-sample predictive power by itself; (ii) the
out-of-sample test is the S8 fresh-law forward rounds (Fubini-analogy
law and regularity-stratification law; predictions registered and
frozen before adjudication, unsealed during review); (iii) any
taxonomy revision triggered by S8 outcomes will be disclosed as a
criterion-evolution event, in the same register as the H31
three-layer attribution.
