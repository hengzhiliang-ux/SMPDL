\subsection{The Preregistered Protocol Chain}\label{sec:val-chain}

Every scored round passes a ten-gate checklist (Appendix~A) before
adjudication: registration commit (R2), zero result-side content in
the registered text (R3), third-party or scripted freeze (F1--F4),
material-package self-assessment physically deleted (M1/M3),
adjudication in a fresh isolated session (J1), frozen preregistered
scoring standard (J2: three questions D1 scale / D2 time $\le$5
years / D3 function, D1-priority, rule~H for immediate emergence,
compound events judged whole), tier separation + baseline sync +
dual-layer $\kappa$ + preregistered reading rule (J3/J4/K2/K3).

\subsection{Adjudicator Independence (\S7.5)}\label{sec:val-independence}

\textbf{Same-lineage disclosure.} All B4-2/B4-3 adjudications were
performed by isolated sessions of the same model lineage as the
registration pipeline; dual $\kappa=1.00$ cannot exclude shared
training bias. This was disclosed at registration time and motivated
B4-4. Inter-rater agreement is interpreted per
\citet{landis1977kappa}; machine-assisted science reproducibility
framing follows \citet{kapoor2023leakage}.

\textbf{Cross-lineage design.} J-A = Kimi (different vendor), J-C =
DeepSeek (different vendor, live web retrieval, process rows
retained as evidence), each re-adjudicated the same 17 frozen rounds
under the same preregistered three-question standard; the
coordinator (same lineage as registration) is barred from judging
(J1 variant) and performed only mechanical steps.

\textbf{Results, both directions.} (i) The direction-discrimination
core of the two-stage law --- \emph{which functional class does the
emergent structure belong to} --- replicates across all three
lineages (pairwise main-type $\kappa$ 0.625--0.854, all
$\ge$0.6). (ii) Three-tier severity does not replicate (0.09--0.32);
every divergence localizes to one of three \emph{named adjudicator
variables}: D2 anchor discipline (historiographic vs rule-based
anchoring; flipped 5 rounds, all in the tier layer, zero in the
main-type layer), $\alpha/\gamma$ main-vs-auxiliary classification
preference (3 rounds), and D1 causal-threshold strictness (J-C's
extreme-strict reading killed 4 rounds at the D1 gate) ---
severity scoring is a judge free parameter, and protocol design must
anchor it via registration fields (S8 revision clause: D1 threshold
explicitly fixed in prompts).

\textbf{Moderator replication (preregistered P1--P5).} The claim
``registration fields, not ruler anchoring, drive tier severity''
was itself forward-tested: P1 (B4-3 rounds with time-tendency
registered reproduce original tiers, 3/3), P4 (no-registration
long-gap rounds cluster at the disagreement edge, 4/5), P5
(main-type agreement $\ge$14/17, observed 12/13 = 92.3\%) \textbf{
hit}; P2 (R2 natural anchor double-❌) and P3 (short-interval rounds
$\ge$5/6) \textbf{missed}, both misses attributing to the newly
visible D1 variable rather than moderator failure --- reported as
registered, E4.

\textbf{R2, rewritten as a boundary specimen.} The Church--Rosser
round was designed as a natural anchor (baseline ``every removal is
$\beta$'' should fail there); the three adjudicators instead split
three ways (original $\alpha$ / J-A $\alpha$ on different grounds /
J-C $\beta$ --- the only one matching the registered prediction).
The round is therefore rewritten in this paper not as an anchor of
agreement but as a \emph{live boundary specimen of the functional
taxonomy itself}: genuine reading ambiguity of what
Church--Rosser's removal functionally is. This rewrite is itself a
criterion-evolution event, disclosed in the same register as H31.

\textbf{External arbitration.} Three historiographic questions
arising from divergent D2 anchors were resolved by literature:
$\pm\pi/4$ traces to \textbf{Cauchy 1814} (\emph{M\'emoire sur les
int\'egrales d\'efinies}; integrand identical to the modern textbook
example; Cauchy invented singular integrals to study exactly this
order-swap failure --- the original anchor line's ``Cauchy 1827
false assertion'' was doubly wrong: year, and role); tropical
mathematics institutionalization anchors at the 1994 Bristol
conference (AMS CONM 616 endmatter + Litvinov--Maslov program
self-description), supporting the dequantization $\gamma$-reading;
and no canonical historiographic anchoring convention exists ---
Corry's body/image framework supplies the operational criterion
(institutionalization anchor fixed in text)
\citep{corry2004modern}, endorsing our
registration-time anchor-type declaration revision; the Fubini
$\pm\pi/4$ counterexample traces to Cauchy 1814
\citep{grattan1997cauchy, gray2015real}, and the iterated-limit
tradition to \citealp{pringsheim1899doppel}. All three
registered as evidence leanings; committed verdicts untouched.

\textbf{Erratum disclosure (E1, type-1 staleness variant).} The
candidate-pool ledger lagged its own consumption by one day
(pool rows marked ``pending'' though consumed by B4-2 rounds),
which mis-led one downstream decision into proposing
already-consumed rounds as fresh samples. Caught by full-repo
scan, fixed same day, and routed into this section as a disclosed
bookkeeping-contamination event; new rule: pool consumption writes
back its status column in the same round.

\textbf{What remains open.} Human adjudication ($\kappa_2$
human-vs-original, $\kappa_3$ human-vs-heterogeneous) is the
standing gap; recruitment (mathematics / history of mathematics,
no prior SMPDL exposure) is ongoing, with the material package
reusing the adjudicator memo. Until then, three-tier conclusions
carry the qualifier ``same-lineage consistent, cross-lineage
divergent'' and main-type conclusions carry ``three-lineage
replicated, no human calibration point yet''.

\textbf{Protocol validation (S8a, 2026-10-19).} The three named
adjudicator variables motivated a revised protocol v2
(preregistered in the S8 workorder, commit \texttt{00902eb};
historiographic anchor methodology per
\citealp{corry2004modern}, with the non-Archimedean case
documented by \citealp{ehrlich2006rise}): D1 causal threshold as
a declared menu with a one-sentence direct-causal-chain
obligation; D2 anchor type fixed by registration-side declaration
(breach anchor; e.g.\ \citealp{veronese1891continuo} for H34);
D3 main type bound by rule (first domain-scale achievement).
Re-adjudicating the same frozen H34 (Archimedes) prediction with
fresh same-vendor-different-session arms --- J-D (Kimi desktop,
\texttt{2424aef}) and J-E (DeepSeek, \texttt{566a70b}), identical
prompt SHA-256 --- the v1 three-tier split
($\checkmark$/$\checkmark$/half for original/J-A/J-C) converged:
both v2 arms scored full hit with identical three declarations
(strict D1; breach anchor 1891 Veronese; rule-bound D3). The

of $\sim$1900$\to$1961/66, cf.\ \citealp{hahn1907nichtarchimedischen,
robinson1961nonstandard}) flips to full hit under the

layer stays $\beta$ across all five observations. Registered

never counted as forward record; residual judge freedom remains
in display-year identification (both arms volunteered
same-direction sensitivity notes: a stricter display standard
would flip D2); J-E disclosed no-realtime-web verification.
Linkage clause frozen: fresh-law forward rounds (S8b, two C-2
laws registered and frozen blind at \texttt{76ab9fc}/
\texttt{9c81083} before any B4-4 unsealing) will be adjudicated
under protocol v2 at review time.
